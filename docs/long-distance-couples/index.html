Why couples love a shared board

Texts scroll away. A note pinned to someone’s desktop stays there all day — every time they glance at their screen, there you are. Corkly gives the two of you one shared corkboard: you each see it on your own computer, and anything either of you pins appears on the other’s screen within seconds.

It works between a Mac and a Windows PC, across any distance or time zone.

How it works

  1. You both download Corkly (free for Mac and Windows).
  2. One of you creates a free account and taps Share on a board.
  3. Send your partner the invite code. They join, and the board appears on their computer too.
  4. Pin away. New things arrive live, with a little “NEW” sticker and a notification.

Free, with a little extra

Sharing one board with your partner is free, and so are games and voice notes up to 30 seconds. Corkly Plus adds more shared boards, room for up to 25 people on each, and voice notes up to 2 minutes.

Questions

Does my partner need the same kind of computer?

No. One of you can use a Mac and the other a Windows PC — shared boards work across both.

Can they see everything on my computer?

No. They only see the board you shared with them. Your other boards stay private on your computer.

Can we use it on our phones?

Corkly is a desktop app for Mac and Windows for now. If you find it on your phone, getcorkly.com can email you the download link for your computer.

Is it free for two people?

Yes. Sharing one board with your partner — including games and voice notes up to 30 seconds — is free.
